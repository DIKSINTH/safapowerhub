Our Mission Statement

Our mission is to accelerate the adoption of Electric Vehicles (EVs) there by making mobility more sustainable. By catalyzing EV adoption we endeavor reduce our nation's dependence on imported oil and make our air cleaner.

Our Belief

We believe that when public charging becomes truly accessible and reliable, the electric mobility revolution will thrive in India.

Building Confidence in Electric Mobility

EV drivers should have the confidence to travel anywhere, knowing there are adequate public charging points across the country.

Charging must be a smooth and dependable process, providing peace of mind for every journey.

When public charging becomes as simple as fueling at a petrol pump, EV adoption will soar, helping India achieve its sustainability goals.

Join The Movement

At Safa Power Hub, We're More Than A Charging Network—We're Building The Future Of Electric Mobility In India. With Every Public Charging Station We Create And Every Driver We Inspire, We Take A Step Closer To A Cleaner, Greener Tomorrow.
